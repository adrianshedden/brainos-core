# BrainOS

BrainOS gives your AI a brain to use and keep adding to, so it just knows you and your work. Your brain is a folder of plain text files on your own computer, readable by any AI.

This public repository shows the structure only: the folders and files a BrainOS brain is made of (see `STRUCTURE.md`). The product itself is not here.

## Get BrainOS

BrainOS comes from Remyn: https://remyn.me/

- Buy it on the Remyn site, then open your setup page. Your download of `BrainOS.zip` is there.
- Attendees of a Remyn session get a code for the same download.

Who makes it: BrainOS is the product, Remyn is the brand, and AI Ops is the company behind both.
