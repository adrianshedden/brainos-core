# What am I putting off

Question: What have I been putting off?
Cadence: weekly
Read: memory/conversations/, memory/calendar/, memory/decisions.jsonl, CLAUDE.md

What to do: find things I said I would do, send or arrange in my own messages in `memory/conversations/`, and decisions in `memory/decisions.jsonl`, that have no sign of being done since. Add any goal in `CLAUDE.md` that nothing in my calendar or conversations has touched lately. Oldest first.
