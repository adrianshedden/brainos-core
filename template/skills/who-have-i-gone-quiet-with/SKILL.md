# Who have I gone quiet with

Question: Who have I gone quiet with?
Cadence: monthly
Read: {{PEOPLE}}, memory/conversations/, CLAUDE.md, skills/draft-in-my-voice/SKILL.md, tools/TOOLS.md

What to do:

1. Find my key people. Start with anyone named as a key person in `CLAUDE.md` (level 1) or in the key people that `mine-my-context` wrote in `network/NETWORK.md`, if the brain has one. Add the people with the most lines in `{{PEOPLE}}interactions.jsonl` over all time (count each line where they are in `with`, `from` or `to`). Refer to each by their `id` in `{{PEOPLE}}people.jsonl`.
2. Last contact. For each, the date of their most recent line in `{{PEOPLE}}interactions.jsonl`, and what it was about: its title or subject, or, if the line names a `file` in `memory/conversations/`, the last few messages there. If `tools/TOOLS.md` lists a mail or calendar connector as on, check it for anything newer before you call someone quiet.
3. Keep only people whose last contact is 60 days or more before today. Rank them by how many lines they have, most first. Show the top 5 unless I ask for more. If nobody is quiet, say so.
4. Why they matter: one line from the files, for example their role in `{{PEOPLE}}people.jsonl`, how often we met, or a goal in `CLAUDE.md` they link to. If nothing says why, say so.
5. A short draft note for each, in my voice. Follow `skills/draft-in-my-voice/SKILL.md`: read messages I wrote myself in `memory/conversations/` and copy my length, greeting, sign-off and tone. Two to four sentences. Open with something specific from our last contact, then offer something light (a coffee, a quick call, something useful). Ask them for nothing. Do not say how long it has been, and do not write "just checking in". If there is too little to make it specific, say so and ask me what to mention, rather than write a general note.

Show your sources: after each person, name the file and date their last contact came from, the file behind "why they matter", and a confidence (high, medium or low). Under each draft, name one or two of my own messages you copied the style from, with their files.
Never: send, post or schedule a note, or contact anyone. Show the drafts only; I send them. Never invent a last contact, a topic or a reason someone matters: if it is not in the files, say so.
