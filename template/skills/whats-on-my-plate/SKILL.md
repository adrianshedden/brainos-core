# What's on my plate

Question: What is on my plate for the next seven days?
Cadence: weekly
Read: memory/calendar/, memory/conversations/, CLAUDE.md, tools/TOOLS.md

What to do: list the meetings in the next seven days (from today) by day from `memory/calendar/events.yaml`, then anything I said I would do in recent conversations. If `tools/TOOLS.md` lists a calendar connector as on, read those seven days from it too (a connected calendar may hold meetings the export does not). Put first whatever matters most to my goals in `CLAUDE.md`. If nothing falls in those seven days, say so and list the next things coming up.
Show your sources: after each point, name the file it came from, for example `memory/calendar/events.yaml`, or the connector.
Never: accept, decline or move a meeting, or contact anyone.
