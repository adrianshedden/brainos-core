# The Claude app's Chat tab

Once Claude can see this folder, tell it: "Read CLAUDE.md first."

## How to point it at your brain

- Open the Claude app on your computer and use the Chat tab: you hand Claude a task from the same message box as a conversation. Cowork, the old name, is now part of Chat; older versions of the app still show a separate Cowork option. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)
- Under the message box, click "Project or folder" and choose this brain folder. (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- When Claude asks "Allow Claude to access this folder?", click "Always allow", or tick "Don't ask again for this folder". (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- It can only read and write files in folders you have connected. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)
- Keep the Claude app open while it works on files on your computer. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)

## What it can do

- Read and save files in this folder directly, with no uploading or downloading. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)
- It sends the files it makes to the connected folder on your computer itself. No add-on is needed. (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- It can use your exports in two ways. Drag them into this brain's `exports` folder, which keeps Claude to the brain folder only. Or leave them in Downloads: when Claude asks "Claude wants to use a folder on your computer", click "Allow once". That lasts this chat only. (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- It asks your permission before permanently deleting any file. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)

## What it cannot do

- It cannot see your Downloads folder unless you allow it. Heads-up: once allowed, Claude can read and change everything in Downloads for that chat. (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- It does not work only on your computer. The app says: "Because this session runs in the cloud, files Claude uses leave your device and are processed on Anthropic's servers." (Source: Claude desktop app on a Mac, seen 15 Oct 2026)
- It is not on the free plan. It needs a paid Claude plan: Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)
- Long, many-step jobs use more of your plan's allowance than a quick question. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork, checked 15 October 2026)
