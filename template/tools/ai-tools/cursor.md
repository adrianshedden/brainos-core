# Cursor

Open this brain folder in Cursor and tell its agent: "Read CLAUDE.md first."

## How to point it at your brain

- Pick a folder to open. It runs on Mac (macOS 12 or later), Windows 10 or later, and Linux. (Source: https://cursor.com/docs/get-started/quickstart, checked 1 October 2026)

## What it can do

- Search your folder and read the files that matter for the question. (Source: https://cursor.com/docs/get-started/quickstart, checked 1 October 2026)
- Suggest edits, apply them and run commands. (Source: https://cursor.com/docs/agent/overview, checked 1 October 2026)
- Undo its changes by restoring a checkpoint. (Source: https://cursor.com/docs/agent/overview, checked 1 October 2026)

## What it cannot do

- Do much for free: the free Hobby plan has limited agent requests, and paid plans start at $20 a month. (Source: https://cursor.com/pricing, checked 1 October 2026)
