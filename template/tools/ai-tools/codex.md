# OpenAI Codex

Open this brain folder in Codex. It reads `AGENTS.md` by itself, and level 1 is at its top.

## How to point it at your brain

- Codex reads `AGENTS.md` in the folder it starts in, and not `CLAUDE.md`, which is why this brain's `AGENTS.md` opens with the same level 1 words. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/agents-md, checked 18 October 2026)
- Codex is part of the ChatGPT desktop app, where you choose the folder to work in. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app, checked 18 October 2026)
- The Codex CLI runs in a terminal on your own computer, and you sign in with your ChatGPT account. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/cli, checked 1 October 2026)
- Codex cloud works on a GitHub repo you connect, such as your brain's private backup. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/cloud, checked 1 October 2026)

## What it can do

- Look at your files, change them and run commands. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/cli, checked 1 October 2026)
- In the cloud, commit its work or open a pull request on the connected repo. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/cloud, checked 1 October 2026)
- It comes with every ChatGPT plan from Free upwards. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/pricing, checked 1 October 2026)

## What it cannot do

- Run without limits on the cheaper plans: Free, Go and Plus are capped per five-hour window, and Pro is not. (Source: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/pricing, checked 1 October 2026)
- Save into your folder from the desktop app. (Not checked: https://learn.chatgpt.com/codex/app says it can use the files in a location you choose, but not whether it writes there.)
