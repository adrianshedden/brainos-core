# Work

My job, my business or ventures, and the projects in them.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `projects.yaml` | One entry per project or venture: name, what it is, status, next step, who is involved (by person id from the network) |

Make `projects.yaml` when there is a first project to add. My LinkedIn positions are in `memory/profile/linkedin-positions.yaml`.

## What goes in

- What I am working on, its status and next step, and decisions about it (decisions also go to `memory/decisions.jsonl`).

## What stays out

- My employer's or clients' confidential documents. Note that they exist and where they live; do not copy them here.
- Passwords and keys.
