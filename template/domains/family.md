# Family

Home and family life: the people at home, family dates and plans, things to remember.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `family.yaml` | Who is in the family (by person id from the network where there is one), birthdays, plans and things to remember |

## What goes in

- What I choose to keep about home life, each with its source.

## What stays out

- Anything a family member would not want written down. Ask first when in doubt.
- Children's school, health or identity documents: note where they are kept, not what is in them.
