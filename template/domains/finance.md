# Finance

My money: what comes in and goes out, bills and renewals, savings goals.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `finance.yaml` | Regular bills and renewals (what, how much, when), and savings goals |

## What goes in

- Figures and dates I choose to note, each with its source and the date it was true.

## What stays out

- Passwords, PINs, full card or account numbers and sign-in codes. Never.
- Advice. An AI here can help me keep track; it is not a financial adviser.
