# Knowledge

Things I know or am learning, kept for reuse: notes, reading, how-tos, references.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `<topic>.md` | One note per topic, in my words |
| `references.yaml` | Books, articles and links I want to keep: title, where, why it mattered |

## What goes in

- Notes and references I asked to keep, each saying where it came from.

## What stays out

- Whole copies of other people's articles or books: keep a link and my notes instead.
- Day-to-day happenings: they belong in `memory/`.

- (Written by `mine-my-context`: topics that come up often, with source and confidence.)
