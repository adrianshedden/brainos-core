# Health

My own health and fitness: habits, goals, appointments I want to remember.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `health.jsonl` | One line per note: date, what, source, confidence |

## What goes in

- What I choose to note about my own health and routines.

## What stays out

- Anyone else's health.
- Medical records and test results: note where they are kept, not what they say.
- Advice. An AI here can help me keep track; it is not a doctor.
