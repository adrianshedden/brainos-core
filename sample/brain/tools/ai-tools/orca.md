# Orca

Orca has no AI of its own: choose one of the agents with its own card (Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode) inside Orca, then follow that card.

## How to point it at your brain

- This card is about Orca at onorca.dev. If you mean a different Orca, it does not apply. (Not checked: we could not confirm which Orca was meant; this is the only AI development app called Orca we found.)
- Your brain needs to be a git repository first, which the BrainOS GitHub guide does for you. (Not checked: our reading of how Orca works, below.)

## What it can do

- Run other AI agents, such as Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, with subscriptions you already have. (Source: https://www.onorca.dev/, checked 1 October 2026)
- Run every task in its own separate git working copy. (Source: https://www.onorca.dev/, checked 1 October 2026)
- It is free and open source. (Source: https://www.onorca.dev/, checked 1 October 2026)

## What it cannot do

- Answer anything by itself: it works only through an agent you plug in. (Source: https://www.onorca.dev/, checked 1 October 2026)
