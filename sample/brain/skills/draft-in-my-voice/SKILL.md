# Draft a message in my voice

Question: Draft a message in my voice.
Cadence: as-needed
Read: memory/conversations/, CLAUDE.md

What to do: before drafting, read messages I wrote myself in `memory/conversations/` (I am the person who appears in every one of my chats; `CLAUDE.md` may give my name and how I like to work) and notice how I write: length, greeting, sign-off, how formal, emoji or none. If the brain has a `memory/profile/tone.md`, follow it. Then draft the message I ask for.
Show your sources: under the draft, name two or three of my own messages you copied the style from, with their files.
Never: send it, and never copy another person's words into my draft. You only write the draft for me to send.
