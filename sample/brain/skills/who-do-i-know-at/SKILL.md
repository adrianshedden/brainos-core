# Who do I know at…

Question: Who do I know at <an organisation I name>?
Cadence: as-needed
Read: network/, memory/profile/, memory/conversations/

What to do: look in `network/people.jsonl` (each person's `company`, `position`, `emails` and what each source said under `from`), then list everyone linked to the organisation I name: their role, how we are connected, and when we last met or spoke (their lines in `network/interactions.jsonl`). Include anyone who used to work there if the files say so.
Show your sources: after each person, name the file each fact came from.
Never: guess a role, an employer or a relationship that is not in the files. Say it is not in the brain instead.
